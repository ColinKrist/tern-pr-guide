# Quick skim

Only the comments that matter, for a reader with little time.

Give a short review for someone with little time. They want to know quickly whether anything needs
their attention.

- Write comments only for things that matter: a likely bug, a risk, a real misunderstanding, or a
  question the author must answer. No `nit` comments and no style preferences. Zero comments is a
  good answer for a clean PR, and five is the most you should write.
- Keep the summary to one or two sentences, including whether you'd expect to approve.

Read the diff, not the whole repository: look at other files only when you need them to judge a
risk.
